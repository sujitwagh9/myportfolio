## Problem

Small farmers usually sell through middlemen, without good visibility of current market prices, while buyers and companies have no simple way to reach them directly.

## What it does

- **Separate farmer and company accounts**, each with its own sign-up, login and session handling.
- **Sell and buy crops** directly on the platform.
- **Live market insights:** a Flask service pulls mandi prices from the Government of India's open data API (data.gov.in), filtered by state, district and commodity.
- **Logistics:** list and book storage space and transport vehicles.
- **Planning tools for farmers:** price statistics, budget planning and government schemes.
- **Company dashboards** for prices and stats, with charts.
- **AI helper** powered by Gemini.

## Architecture

- **Frontend and API routes:** Next.js (App Router) with Tailwind, Framer Motion and Recharts.
- **Backend:** a Node.js and Express service with MongoDB, JWT auth and bcrypt.
- **Market data service:** Flask, calling the data.gov.in API.

## Stack

Next.js · Node.js · Express · MongoDB · Flask · Gemini

## Outcome

A three-service platform that puts market prices, buyers and logistics in one place for farmers.
