## Problem

Choosing where to place dark stores across a city means balancing customer demand against rent and running costs.

## Architecture

- Customer demand locations are **clustered** to find natural service areas.
- **Voronoi maps** are generated from candidate store locations to show the area each store would serve.
- Average **rental and operational expenditure** is estimated from economic data.
- A **Flask API** returns expected expenditure for a given set of coordinates and budget, consumed by a Next.js front end. Data is stored in MongoDB.
