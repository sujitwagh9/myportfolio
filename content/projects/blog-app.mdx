## What it does

- **User management:** register, log in, and manage roles (reader, author, admin).
- **Articles:** create, read, update and delete, with access restricted by role.
- **Authentication** with JWT access and refresh tokens.
- **Password reset** by email (Nodemailer with Gmail SMTP).

## Stack

Node.js · Express · MongoDB · JWT · Nodemailer

## Outcome

A backend for a multi-role blog, deployed on Render.
