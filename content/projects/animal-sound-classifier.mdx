## What it does

- **Upload a sound recording** through a Flask web app.
- **Audio to image:** the recording is turned into a spectrogram with librosa.
- **Classify:** a MobileNetV2-based TensorFlow model predicts the bird species from the spectrogram.
- **Distress check:** the sound's frequency profile is compared against typical values for that species to flag possible pain or distress.

## Stack

Python · TensorFlow · librosa · Flask

## Outcome

An end-to-end audio classification app that reuses a vision model by treating sound as an image.
