## What it does

- **Accounts:** register, log in and log out, with email verification links, and forgot/reset password.
- **Role-based access** for admins, instructors and students.
- **Courses:** instructors upload courses; students enrol and access them.
- **Payments:** Razorpay integration to create and verify payments, with webhook handling for payment notifications.
- **Secure sessions** with JWT access and refresh tokens, and bcrypt password hashing.
- **Validation and errors:** Joi validation and centralised error-handling middleware.

## Stack

Node.js · Express · MongoDB (Mongoose) · JWT · bcrypt · Nodemailer · Razorpay · Joi
