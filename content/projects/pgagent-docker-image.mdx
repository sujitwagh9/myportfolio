## Problem

Automating the data pipeline required a reliable job scheduler inside a fully Dockerised platform that hosts **multiple databases**.

## What I tried first

I started with **pg_cron**, a cron-style scheduling extension for PostgreSQL, and modified the PostgreSQL Dockerfile to include it. Once it was running, I hit a critical limitation: pg_cron works on a **single database per cluster**, which doesn't fit a multi-database platform.

## The fix

**pgAgent** is a job scheduler built for PostgreSQL that handles this case. But it had **no official Docker support**: it's normally installed directly on the host, and there was no maintained community image.

Rather than treat that as a blocker, I built our own image from scratch. I studied the official pgAgent documentation and its source code, and iterated through trial and error until the container worked reliably with the rest of the platform.

## Stack

pgAgent · PostgreSQL · Docker · Linux

## Outcome

A fully functional, containerised pgAgent that schedules pipeline jobs across the platform's databases. It's one of my most significant technical achievements, solved entirely through independent research.
